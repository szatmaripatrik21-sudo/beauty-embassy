Coastal & Natural

Coastal & Natural. Salon Interior Design Inspiration


YOUR SALON DESIGN STYLE:   

natural, greenery with a beachy feel.

Through the use of natural light, soft tones, and a clean aesthetic, this salon look evokes the breeziness of the beach combined with a natural organic feel.

Draw on elements of nature, embracing light timber salon furniture and architectural details, incorporate rattan and greenery, while keeping it clean and minimal in style. 

White is key to the natural coastal colour palette.
Creating a soft base will allow you to make way for decorative pieces.
